## Platform

web

## Users

- **Swamp Club members**, arriving from the weekly Discord announcement, who
  scan what changed in the swamp extension registry this week and open a few
  extensions that look useful.
- **Extension authors**, checking their own entry and how it was summarized.
- **Agents and scripts**, reading the JSON copy of each issue, `index.json`
  and `llms.txt`.
- The owner reads it first; the community is the wider audience.

## Product Purpose

Last Week in Swamp Club is a weekly digest of the swamp extension registry: new
extensions, updates, pre-releases, yanks and removals, each with a short
machine-written summary of the change, plus that week's Swamp Club podcast
episode and community feed posts. One dated issue per Monday-to-Sunday week,
published the following Monday. Success is a reader who finds the week's
interesting changes in a couple of minutes and clicks through to the
registry.

## Positioning

It is built from the registry's own version history and real code diffs,
not from announcements: an update that only bumps a version string is
counted as minor, and an update that changes an API is called out. Nothing
else gives a week-by-week view of the whole registry.

## Operating Context

- Generated by the `@sntxrr/registry-digest` swamp extension and published
  to GitHub Pages at `https://lastweekinswamp.club`.
- Readers arrive mostly from a Discord link, on desktop and phone.
- A typical issue is long: ~80 new extensions, ~100 updated lines, family
  groups, pre-releases, a minor-update count, a podcast episode and ~5 feed
  posts.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static HTML rendered from TypeScript (`extensions/models/_lib/render.ts`):
  issue page, index page, RSS, JSON copies, `llms.txt`, sitemap.
- **Content-Security-Policy:** `default-src 'none'; style-src 'self';
  img-src 'self'; font-src 'self'`. No scripts, no inline styles, no
  third-party fonts, images or frames. Every page links one shared
  `/site.css`, so a style change reaches back issues; published pages are
  never rebuilt, so asset files are never renamed or deleted.
- All registry and community text is untrusted and escaped; summaries are
  machine-written and labelled as such by a disclaimer on every page.
- Light and dark modes are both required (owner, 2026-10-26).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **Last Week in Swamp Club**.
- Visual reference (owner, 2026-10-26): the owner's artwork *Swamp Synthwave
  Gathering at Dusk*: a dusk bayou of cypress and Spanish moss, an orange
  sunset on still water, amber firefly lights, violet glowing flowers, cyan
  device screens and synthesizer gear. It may be used as a banner on the
  public site and as the source of mood and palette.
- What would feel wrong (owner): hard to scan; generic neon-on-black; heavy
  images or effects that are slow on a phone; a timid recolour that loses
  the artwork's character.

## Product Principles

1. Scanning comes first: the week's changes must be findable in minutes.
2. Describe changes, never judge quality.
3. Everything a reader sees is also available to agents as data.
4. Weight belongs to the content, not the chrome: fast on a phone.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

`prefers-reduced-motion` and `prefers-color-scheme`.
